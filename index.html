Abstract

I am a Predoctoral Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Roorkee, where I research full-time under Prof. Vinay Sharma. Our work brings AI methods to problems in management. I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Business Systems at B V Raju Institute of Technology in 2026. My research interests lie at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Systems Engineering, with a particular focus on Responsible and Safe AI, Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and Meta Research.

Before joining IIT Roorkee, I worked as a Research Apprentice at the Precog Lab, IIIT Hyderabad, under Prof. Ponnurangam "PK" Kumaraguru, and earlier as a Cloud Services Architect designing secure and scalable AWS architectures. I am passionate about bringing positive change to the world through technology and aim to pursue graduate studies in Computer Science with a focus on AI research.
